01

INFORMATION ON THE SWPS UNIVERSITY AI POLICY

Why do we need the AI Traffic Light Model?

The model clearly defines the boundary between independent intellectual work and permitted technological support. It protects cognitive integrity while developing the skills needed for responsible work with generative AI.

01
[●]

RED LEVEL

AI prohibited

Fully independent work, completed either offline or in a controlled environment without access to the internet or AI. It supports durable learning, memory training, and mastery of core skills.

02
[●●]

YELLOW LEVEL

AI support / scaffolding

AI may act as an assistant, sparring partner, or brainstorming tool. The student remains responsible for the claims, substantive decisions, and final work.

03
[●●●]

GREEN LEVEL

AI integration

The use of AI is encouraged or required and forms part of the learning outcomes, for example in prompt engineering, multimedia, graphics, or data processing.

Assess the process, not only the product.

At the Yellow and Green levels, instructors may require an AI-use declaration, prompt history, and an oral substantive defence (viva voce).

METHOD CLASSIFICATION RULES

Decision matrix — examples

The examples below may help you describe assessment tasks in a syllabus — or you may adapt them completely. Match the level to what the method is actually intended to assess. One method may combine several levels; if so, explain the boundaries of each use.

Assessment methodLevelRationale
Final examinations[●] RedAssessment of knowledge and recall in a controlled environment without internet access.
Tests and entrance quizzes[●] RedProtection of independent reading, comprehension, and mastery of key concepts.
Statistics homework[●] RedIndependent mastery of test selection, software operation, and interpretation of results.
E-learning quizzes[●] RedIndividual assessment: AI must not solve the test on the student's behalf.
Class participation and workshop activities[●●] Yellow / RedDuring class: independent work. Before class: AI may help generate arguments and scenarios.
Peer assessment[●] RedAI must not be used to assess people; judgments must be based on personal observation and responsibility.
Individual projects and essays[●●] Yellow / RedAI may support research, language, and APA 7 formatting. Hypotheses, conclusions, and self-reflection must be the student's own.
Case studies and short-form communications[●●] YellowAI may help generate alternatives; the student must provide a scholarly rationale and defend the chosen solution.
Group projects and multimedia[●●●] Green / YellowAI may create media and support editing; disclosure of AI use and labelling of synthetic content are required.
Scientific poster and presentation[●●●] Green / Yellow / RedAI may support graphic design and presentation preparation; the oral presentation must be delivered independently.

Practical test: [●] RED LEVEL >> No form of AI is permitted. The assessment verifies practical skills, independent test selection, and the ability to use statistical software. Homework: [●] RED LEVEL >> AI use is prohibited. Homework directly prepares students for the test and consolidates knowledge; analyses must be completed independently.
Personal Skills+
Workshop activities: [●●] YELLOW/RED LEVEL >> Tasks completed during class are fully independent and AI-free (Red). Before class, AI may support brainstorming (Yellow). Case study: [●●] YELLOW/RED LEVEL >> AI may support refinement of situation descriptions (Yellow). Substantive reasoning remains entirely the responsibility of the students (Red). Individual project: [●●] YELLOW/RED LEVEL >> AI may be used for language correction (Yellow). Self-assessment and development planning require personal reflection (Red).

Study implementation and report: [●●] YELLOW/RED LEVEL >> AI may suggest methods; analyses, results, and conclusions must be original. Scientific poster: [●●●] GREEN/YELLOW/RED LEVEL >> AI may generate visual design and help condense the text; the oral presentation must be delivered independently. Peer assessment: [●] RED LEVEL >> AI must not be used to evaluate other people's work.
Work and Organisational Psychology+
Written examination: [●] RED LEVEL >> A knowledge test completed under controlled conditions without access to AI. Class tests: [●] RED LEVEL >> Knowledge assessments completed entirely independently during class. Assessment tasks: [●●●] GREEN/YELLOW LEVEL >> AI may assist in developing an intervention concept (Yellow) and generating advertising copy (Green). A substantive defence is required.
Master's Seminar 1–4+
Stage I — research plan: [●●] YELLOW/RED LEVEL >> AI may support the initial literature review (Yellow). Hypotheses must be formulated by the student on the basis of their own knowledge (Red). Stage IV — thesis submission: [●●] YELLOW/RED LEVEL >> AI may support language correction and a preliminary formatting check (Yellow). Full substantive independence and knowledge of all sources are required (Red).

Written class test: [●] RED LEVEL >> The test assesses independent analysis of course materials, legislation, and case law. AI is prohibited. Only materials specified by the instructor may be used. AI is prohibited.
